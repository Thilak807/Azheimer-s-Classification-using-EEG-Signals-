About the EEG Alzheimer's Detection Project

Understanding the neuroscience, deep learning architecture, and evaluation methodology.

1. Problem & Clinical Motivation

Alzheimer's Disease (AD) is a progressive neurodegenerative condition characterized by cortical atrophy and synaptic loss. Scalp Electroencephalography (EEG) records the summation of postsynaptic potentials from millions of pyramidal neurons, providing real-time electrophysiological insights:

AD (Alzheimer's Disease)

Pathological "EEG slowing" characterized by excessive low-frequency Delta (0.5–4 Hz) and Theta (4–8 Hz) spectral power and severe reduction in dominant posterior Alpha waves.

HC (Healthy Control)

Normal cognitive aging with strong, synchronized posterior Alpha rhythm (8–13 Hz) during resting eyes-closed recordings.

FTD (Frontotemporal Dementia)

Focal neurodegeneration targeting anterior frontal and temporal electrodes (Fp1, Fp2, F3, F4, Fz, T3, T4) with localized spectral frequency shifts.

2. Why Hybrid CNN-LSTM?

Standard classifiers (like standalone SVM or KNN) require manual feature engineering and struggle with raw multichannel temporal dependencies. Our Hybrid architecture combines the strengths of both paradigms:

1D CNN Spatial/Morphological Extractor

Hierarchical 1D convolutions with multi-scale receptive fields (filters=64, 128; kernels=7, 5, 3) scan across the 19 channels simultaneously, learning spatial wave morphologies and spectral peak features.

Bidirectional LSTM Sequence Learner

Bidirectional recurrent units process the extracted spatial feature maps across time in both forward and backward directions, capturing long-range temporal rhythms and state transitions.

3. Evaluation Metrics Explained

Accuracy: The ratio of correctly predicted EEG windows over the total evaluated windows across all diagnostic classes.
Macro Precision: The average percentage of predicted cases for each class that were truly positive, penalizing false alarms.
Macro Recall: The average sensitivity across all classes, ensuring that true Alzheimer's cases are not missed.
Macro F1-Score: The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall, providing a balanced single metric under class imbalance.
