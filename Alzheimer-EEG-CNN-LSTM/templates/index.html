System Architecture & Pipeline Features

End-to-end framework integrating multichannel EEG data, 5-stage preprocessing with ICA, CNN-LSTM, and PSO optimization.

1 & 2. Preprocessing & ICA

Complete 5-stage pipeline: 0.5–45 Hz bandpass filtering, 50 Hz notch filter, Artifact Removal via ICA (Independent Component Analysis), 5s epoch segmentation, and Z-score normalization.

  • 19-Channel 10-20 Standard Layout
  • FastICA Ocular/Muscle Decontamination

3B & 4B. CNN-LSTM Modeling

Multi-scale 1D CNN layers extract spatial and morphological EEG features, while Bi-LSTM sequence units capture temporal transitions and long-range electrophysiological memory.

  • 3B: Spatial-Temporal Feature Extractor
  • 4B: Bi-LSTM Temporal Modeling

7. Output & 8. PSO Tuning

Classifies early disease stages (CN, MCI, AD). Uses Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to automatically discover optimal learning rate, filters, units, and dropout.

  • 3-Stage Early Disease Classification
  • Particle Swarm Swarm Hyperparameter Tuning
